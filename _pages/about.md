Highly motivated Computer Science graduate with industry experience as a Software Engineer. Passionate about Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Software Engineering, Human-Computer Interaction, and Natural Language Processing. Determined to pursue a post-graduate specialization in the field of Computer Science.

With over five years of professional experience building backend and distributed systems, he is now looking to deepen his research foundation through graduate study.

He completed his B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at [North South University](http://www.northsouth.edu/) with Magna Cum Laude, 2<sup>nd</sup> highest academic distinction, and his M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at [United International University](https://www.uiu.ac.bd/).

Alongside his studies, he has served as a Graduate and Undergraduate Teaching Assistant at the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering](http://ece.northsouth.edu/), North South University. At United International University, he worked as a Research Assistant.

Since 2020 he has worked as a software engineer, most recently as a Senior Software Engineer at [InstantFunding](https://instantfunding.com/?partner=8764&gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=24235953353&gbraid=0AAAABEsYhsowkjdCIre9rXW1OBRazCg6c&gclid=Cj0KCQjwuJLWBhD_ARIsAIBcRUyYU9olWZBBG6bDIoWbgJHtElXwSaSnOgy-8HmoMCfLqpnOWvBA7V4aAs7rEALw_wcB), and previously at Pathao Ltd.
